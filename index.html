Story video control center

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Version 12

Stories

Paste your stories here. Put a line with only --- between them. The first line of each story is its title. Each story becomes one video and is never reused.

Background videos

Add 3 to 5 clips you own or have rights to. Each new video uses a random clip from a random start point.

    Voice and captions

    A sample plays when you pick a voice or speed. The first one takes longer while the voice model downloads.

    Generate

    Makes a video for every new story in the box. Stories longer than the max length are split into parts automatically.
